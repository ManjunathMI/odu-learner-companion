# About ODU

Learn anything. Together.

ODU Learner Companion helps students, working professionals, and lifelong learners find a learning goal and make steady progress with other people.

## You do not have to learn alone

A list of resources is only a starting point. A clear plan helps you decide what to study next. A shared Learning Space gives that plan a home where people can follow it together.

## How ODU works

1. [Explore Learning Paths](/explore) for a goal you care about.
2. Sign in with a link or code sent to your email. No password is needed.
3. Request to join a Learning Space, or create a Learning Path for your own group.
4. Follow the lessons, mark your progress, and keep Personal Notes.
5. Return to [My Journey](/journey) when you are ready to continue.

## Learning Paths and Learning Spaces

A Learning Path is the plan: phases, days, and learning resources. A Learning Space is where members follow that plan and see Community Progress.

## What is available today

You can discover paths, request membership, follow lessons, track completion, keep private notes, and create and manage your own paths. AI assistance, discussions, study sessions, and badges are not available as finished features yet.

## Start with one useful step

Choose a goal and [find a Learning Path](/explore). If you need help, [read the getting-started guide](/docs/how-to-use).
