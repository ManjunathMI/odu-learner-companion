# Account and sign-in help

Use your email to sign in, and keep your learning profile up to date.

## I have not received a sign-in email

Check your spam folder and confirm that you entered the right address. Wait before requesting another email; repeated requests may be limited. Always use the newest link.

## My sign-in link expired

Return to [Sign In](/auth) and request a fresh link. Open it in the same browser where you requested it.

## My join request is pending

A facilitator needs to review your request. Pending membership does not give access to member-only lessons. Check My Journey again after approval.

## A Learning Space is unavailable

A private space requires the appropriate membership. If you believe you should have access, contact the person who manages that space.

## Update your profile

Open **Profile** from your account menu to edit your display name, avatar, bio, and links. Review the profile visibility setting before saving.

## Review your account

Open **Manage Account** to see your path roles, spaces you lead, and creator capacity. These roles can differ from one Learning Space to another.
