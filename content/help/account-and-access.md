# Account and sign-in help

ODU uses email sign-in, so there is no password to remember. Your account menu also gives you access to your profile and account details.

## Sign in with your email

1. Open [Sign In](/auth) and enter your email address.
2. Choose **Send sign-in code**.
3. Check your email. Open the newest sign-in link in the same browser, or enter the verification code if your email includes one.

If you started from a Learning Path, you should return to it after signing in. Otherwise, you will go to [My Journey](/journey).

## If sign-in does not work

### I did not receive an email

Check that the address is correct and look in your spam folder. Give the email a little time to arrive before asking for another. Use the newest link or code; repeated requests may be limited.

### My link has expired

Return to [Sign In](/auth) and request a new email. Open the latest link in the same browser where you started.

### The code was not accepted

Make sure you entered the code from the newest email. If it still does not work, request another sign-in email and try again.

## Manage your profile

Choose **Profile** from the account menu to edit your display name, avatar, short bio, and social or repository links. The **Profile visibility** setting controls who can see that information: **Joined paths only** is the default, or you can choose **Public**. Choose **Save profile** when you are done.

## Review your account

Open [Manage Account](/account) to see the Learning Paths you lead or have joined, your path roles, and your creator capacity. Your role can be different in each Learning Space.

## Common questions

### My join request is pending. Can I open the lessons?

Not yet. The Space Creator needs to approve your request first. You can check the request in My Journey and open the path after it is approved.

### Why can I see a path but not its learning plan?

Public path overviews can be browsed before joining. Member lessons are available after your membership is approved. Private paths require an invitation or the right membership.

### Does making my profile public make my notes public too?

No. Profile visibility and Personal Notes are separate. Your notes remain private to you.
