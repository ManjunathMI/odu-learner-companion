# Get started with ODU

Choose something you want to learn, find a Learning Path, and take your first step.

## Find a Learning Path

1. Open [Explore](/explore).
2. Search for a topic or select a tag.
3. Open a path to read its overview.

You can browse approved public paths before signing in.

## Sign in without a password

1. Select **Sign In** and enter your email address.
2. Select **Send sign-in code**.
3. Open the newest sign-in link in the browser where you requested it. If the email includes a code, you can enter that instead.

You will return to your selected learning page or to My Journey.

## Join a Learning Space

Open the path and use its join action. If approval is required, your request stays pending until a facilitator reviews it. Once approved, open it from [My Journey](/journey).

## Begin learning

Open a lesson resource, work through it, and mark the lesson complete when you are ready. Your saved progress helps you continue next time.

## Keep going

Return to My Journey for your spaces and pending requests. Read [Learning and Personal Notes](/docs/learning-and-notes) for more help.
