# Start your first learning journey

Welcome to ODU. Start with something you would like to learn. You can look around first and sign in when you are ready to join a learning community or save your progress.

## 1. Find a Learning Path

Open [Explore](/explore) and search for a subject, or choose a topic tag. Open a path to read its overview and decide whether it fits your goal.

You can browse approved public paths without an account. A **Learning Path** is the plan: it groups lessons into phases and days. A **Learning Space** is the shared place around that plan, where its members learn together.

## 2. Sign in when you are ready

Choose **Sign In** and enter your email address. ODU will email you a sign-in link and, when available, a verification code. Open the newest link in the same browser where you started, or enter the code on the sign-in page. You do not need a password.

If you started from a Learning Path, you will return to it after signing in. Otherwise, you will go to [My Journey](/journey).

## 3. Ask to join a Learning Space

On the path page, choose **Request to join**. Sign in first if asked. Your request will appear as pending in [My Journey](/journey) while the Space Creator reviews it. Once you are approved, open the path from My Journey to see its learning plan.

## 4. Work through the plan

Open a day and choose a lesson or resource. Take the time you need; you can return later and continue where you left off. When you finish a lesson, mark it complete. Your progress saves automatically, and the space shows how far you have come.

For more on lesson resources and private notes, see [Learning and Personal Notes](/docs/learning-and-notes).

## 5. Save a Personal Note

Use the notes area in a lesson to keep a question, idea, or reminder. Choose **Save note** before leaving the lesson. Your notes are private to you. [Learn more about notes and progress](/docs/learning-and-notes).

## 6. Create a path of your own

Want to lead a learning journey for yourself or a group? In My Journey, choose **Create a Learning Path**. Add an overview, then build the plan from phases, days, lessons, and resource links. New paths are private by default. You can change visibility in settings later; a public path needs Platform Admin approval before it appears in Explore. [Have a question about a publication review? Contact us through Feedback & help](#feedback) and include the path title.

Follow the [step-by-step path creation guide](/docs/create-a-learning-space). The default creator allowance is three paths. Joining someone else's path does not use that allowance; you can see your usage in [Manage Account](/account).

## 7. Make your profile yours

Open **Profile** from the account menu to update your display name, photo, bio, and links. Your profile is visible only to people in paths you have joined unless you choose **Public**. For sign-in or access help, see [Account and sign-in help](/docs/account-and-access).

## Common questions

### Can I explore before I sign in?

Yes. Approved public paths are available on Explore. You need to sign in to request to join or take part in a Learning Space.

### Do I need a password?

No. Sign in with the link or verification code sent to your email. If a link has expired, request a new one and use the latest email.

### Why is my join request still pending?

The Space Creator needs to review it. You can check its status in My Journey. Member-only lessons become available after approval.

### Who can read my Personal Notes?

Only you can read your notes in ODU. They are not shared with other learners, Space Creators, or Platform Admins.

### If I make a path public, will it show up on Explore right away?

No. A path starts private. If you change it to public, a Platform Admin must approve it before it appears on Explore. [Ask us about a review](#feedback) if you need help.

### How many paths can I create?

The default allowance is three paths you create. Joining another person's path does not count toward it. Open Manage Account to check your usage or request a higher limit.
