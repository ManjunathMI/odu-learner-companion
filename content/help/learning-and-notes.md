# Learning and Personal Notes

Use your Learning Space to follow a plan, record progress, and capture what you learn.

## Follow the learning plan

Open a space from [My Journey](/journey). The plan groups lesson resources into phases and days. Open each resource to study it at your own pace.

## Track completion

Mark a lesson complete when you have finished it. Reopen the space to continue from your saved progress. Community Progress shows completion for members who have access to the space.

## Write Personal Notes

Use the notes area on a lesson to record an idea, question, or reminder. Save your note before leaving. Your saved notes are available when you return to that lesson.

## Who can read my notes?

Personal Notes are visible only to you through the application. Other learners, facilitators, and platform administrators do not receive access to your notes through the learning interface.

## If something does not save

Check for an error message and retry. If your session has expired, sign in again. Avoid closing the page while a save is still in progress.
