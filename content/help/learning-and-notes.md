# Learn, track progress, and take notes

Once you have joined a Learning Space, its plan helps you know what to work on next. You can study at your own pace and return to pick up where you left off.

## Open your learning plan

Go to [My Journey](/journey) and open a path you have joined. The plan is arranged into phases and days. Open a day to see its lesson material and resource links. Choose a resource by its title to open it in a new tab.

Some days include an explanation or practice activity as well as links. If a section has not been added, use the listed resources or ask the Space Creator about the plan.

## Save your progress

When you finish a lesson, select its checkbox. ODU saves the change automatically. Your progress is updated in the Learning Space and is there when you return.

If you see an error, the checkbox returns to its previous state. Try again; if your session has expired, sign in again first.

## Write a Personal Note

Open **Personal notes** within a lesson, write your note, and choose **Save note**. You can return to that lesson later to read your saved note or add another one.

Your notes are private to you. Other learners, Space Creators, and Platform Admins cannot read them through ODU.

## See Community Progress

The **Community Progress** view shows completion totals for members of the Learning Space. It is separate from your Personal Notes; your notes are never shown there.

## Common questions

### Do I have to finish a day before moving on?

No. Follow the plan in the order that works for you. Your checked lessons stay saved so you can continue later.

### Do I need to press Save after checking a lesson?

No. Lesson completion saves automatically. Personal Notes are different: choose **Save note** after writing one.

### Can other members read my notes?

No. Personal Notes are visible only to you in ODU.
